# Move or fork to independent organization

## Issue
This project is community-owned and a proposal is made to reflect that by moving or forking to an independent organization with ownership shared between interested individuals and organizations. Related issue #928.

## Decision
Transfer the repository from NSIDC/earthaccess to the independent GitHub organization `earthaccess-dev`. The repository will be hosted at https://github.com/earthaccess-dev/earthaccess.

## Status
Accepted

## Assumptions
- The current host organization (NSIDC) will cooperate with the transfer of the repository.

## Constraints
- Minimizing disruption to the user community and preserving existing links, issues, and discussion history.
- Retaining continuity of development and community engagement.

## Positions
- One participant initially proposed creating a fork under a new org (earth-observation-community) and later created the earthaccess-dev org.
- Another participant questioned membership of the proposed org and suggested an alternative org name (earthaccess-contrib).
- Several participants preferred transferring (moving) the entire repository to a neutral organization to keep issues, discussions, and automatic redirects intact, arguing that forking would cause significant disruption.
- Some participants advocated for exploring collaboration with the EODAG project as a parallel or long-term path, but not as a replacement for the immediate move.
- One participant suggested joining an existing community org such as pangeo-data or cloudnativegeo, but after considering governance concerns the community opted to create its own org.
- A participant from the Pangeo steering council expressed willingness to help find a new home.

## Argument
The community reached a consensus to move the repository rather than fork. Moving preserves the project’s history, issues, and discussions, and GitHub automatically redirects links from the old location. This avoids the high transition cost and community disruption that forking would cause. Creating the dedicated `earthaccess-dev` organization provides a neutral governance space that can accommodate future related tools (like python_cmr) and shared documentation, while insulating the project from arbitrary external changes.

## Implications
- The repository is now located at https://github.com/earthaccess-dev/earthaccess.
- Existing links to NSIDC/earthaccess will automatically redirect to the new location.
- The project operates under an independent community governance, with a formal decision document recorded at `docs/governance/decisions/929-move-repository.md`.
- The community can add maintainers more flexibly and group efforts with related tools.
- Future contributions from NASA or other organizations can continue without ownership control.
